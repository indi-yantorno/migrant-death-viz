Recorded deaths in the El Paso Sector have risen sharply since 2019

\

How have annual recorded deaths among migrants in the El Paso sector evolved over the years, and what specific trends can inform resource allocation and strategic planning for humanitarian efforts?

\

\ \

Trend insights

\
\
\

Under 20

\

2008 to 2018: a low baseline

\

Annual recorded deaths stayed below 20 for a decade, peaking at 17 in 2018. At this scale, responses can be handled case by case.

\
\
\

+153%

\

2019: the turning point

\

Deaths rose from 17 to 43 in a single year and have not returned to the earlier baseline, marking a clear break in the pattern.

\
\
\

193

\

2021 to 2024: sustained acceleration

\

Annual deaths passed 100 in 2022 and reached 193 in 2024, about 11 times the 2018 level.

About the data. El Paso Sector Migrant Deaths dataset (UNM Office of the Medical Investigator and partner sources). Counts are case records per year pronounced, after removing blank rows.

\

Limits. Recorded deaths only; remains never found are not counted, so the true number is likely higher. 2025 and 2026 are left out because those years are incomplete (records are added with a delay).
